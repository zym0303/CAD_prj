Basically just fill the lines that have ??? form TwoStage.cir to the output files:
Transient. Replace the ??? under the tran comment with:

spice
wrdata tran_TwoStage.csv v(net6)

This writes the columns time v(net6).

AC. Replace the ??? under the ac comment with:

spice
wrdata ac_TwoStage.csv v(net6)

v(net6) is complex in AC, so this writes frequency real imag.

Noise. There are two ??? lines here. Replace them with:

spice
noise v(net6) vinput dec 10 1 1T
wrdata noise_TwoStage.csv onoise_total

This uses the same sweep as the AC analysis: 10 points per decade, from 1 Hz to 1 THz.

DC. Replace the ??? under the op comment with:

spice
wrdata dc_TwoStage.csv i(vdd)
